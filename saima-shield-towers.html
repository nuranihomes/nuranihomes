How payment works

Paid in stages over about four years.

The developer’s plan spreads the price across the build, so most of it is paid in monthly and quarterly instalments rather than up front.

  1. Booking, allocation and confirmation payments
  2. A payment at the start of construction
  3. 42 monthly instalments
  4. 14 quarterly instalments
  5. Payments linked to construction milestones
  6. A final payment on possession

Corner, west-open, road-facing and park-facing apartments carry an extra charge. Utility connections and documentation are charged separately. We will publish confirmed prices only once the project is cleared for marketing in the UAE.
