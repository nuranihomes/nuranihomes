# Compliance blockers for owner and legal review

Status: pre-launch, 2026-10-22. No licences, registrations, developer mandates, project approvals or overseas marketing permissions were supplied/verified. This is an evidence checklist, not a legal conclusion. Do not activate property-specific lead generation or publish this branch until the relevant approvals and current copy are reviewed.

## Existing content requiring a decision

`index.html`, `buyers.html` and `lang.js` contain project-specific DHA City Karachi positioning, DHA-titled land/ownership assertions, Dubai sales and USD/AED instalment messaging, indicative layouts/prices and buyer eligibility statements. These are existing source claims, not facts established by this audit. The branch preserves visible business copy and removes the unverified land/payment description from Organization schema. An interest-only/no-payment disclaimer does not by itself resolve advertising permission questions.

`privacy.html` states that responses are securely stored in company Google Workspace with team-only access. Backend ownership, access, location and retention were not verified. The buyer notice says information is never shared; counsel must reconcile that language with actual service providers and cross-border processing. Review both English and Urdu before publishing any correction.

## Official sources checked

- [DLD Real Estate Ad Permit](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/real-estate-ad-permit/) lists electronic advertisements and promotional campaigns in its permit service. It does not establish that Nurani has permission or settle the Karachi scenario. Obtain an explicit applicability decision.
- [DLD Trakheesi user guide](https://dubailand.gov.ae/media/m3dhpakj/trakheesi-user-guide_eng.pdf), November 2022, distinguishes company-services promotion from property/project promotion. Confirm current procedure with DLD; do not treat a service-only permit as project approval.
- [UAE government data-protection overview](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws.) describes personal-data processing controls and cross-border transfer requirements. Counsel must determine applicable regime/exemptions and lawful basis for this entity and flow.
- [SBCA sale/advertisement project-information notice](https://www.sbca.gos.pk/pdf/SBCA_Half_Page_Eng_urdu_Final_2020.pdf) and [SBCA model agreement](https://www.sbca.gos.pk/forms/Model%20Agreement.pdf) reference sale/advertisement NOCs. These are authority-level sources, not evidence that this project is approved or that SBCA alone has jurisdiction over its site.

Release evidence must identify the reviewer, date, exact approved wording/assets and permit scope. No property-specific ad, purchase, document submission or customer communication was performed.
