How it works

Buying in Karachi from abroad, without the guesswork.

  1. 01

    We vet every project

    Before we recommend a home, we check the developer’s track record, the land title and the building approvals.

  2. 02

    You choose, with clear advice

    See the full price, every charge, the instalment schedule and the expected handover date up front, in writing. No hidden costs.

  3. 03

    Pay the developer directly

    You pay the developer on their instalment plan while the building goes up. We never hold your money, and no bank loan is needed.

  4. 04

    We handle the paperwork

    Our team in Karachi handles the sale agreement and the transfer into your name, and keeps you updated on progress.

Our standards

Every project we recommend has to pass these checks.

We would rather recommend fewer projects than the wrong ones. If a project fails any of these, we don’t recommend it.

  • Building plans approved by the relevant Karachi authority
  • Clear land title that we verify ourselves
  • A trusted developer with completed, lived-in buildings
  • Clear, transparent pricing: the full price and instalment schedule in writing, with no hidden charges
  • High-quality finishes, specified in writing before you buy
  • Affordable prices, within reach of working families

Why off-plan

A home in Karachi, paid for over the build.

Off-plan means you buy before the building is finished and pay in instalments as it is built, which puts a good home within reach of a monthly salary.

Spread the cost

Instalments during construction instead of one large payment up front.

For family, or for later

A home for your parents, for your own return to Karachi, or to rent out.

Questions

Is NuraniHomes licensed?

We are setting up NuraniHomes as a real estate consultancy in Dubai. Until our licence is in place, we are only collecting interest, and nothing on this site is an offer to sell.

Who are your developers?

We work with established Karachi developers, including a leading developer we partner with on projects tailored to buyers abroad. Projects we are reviewing, such as Saima Shield Towers, are listed above. We will publish full details, prices and approvals once a project is cleared.

Do I need to travel to Karachi?

No. We meet you in Dubai or by video call, and our Karachi team handles the paperwork. You are welcome to visit the site whenever you like.

Do you sell property or take payments?

No. We advise you and support you through the purchase, but you buy from the developer and pay them directly, under a written agreement with them.

How do payments work?

Each project has its own instalment plan, set by the developer and given to you in writing before you commit.
