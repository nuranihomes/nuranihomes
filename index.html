Our standards

Every home we offer has to pass these checks.

We would rather offer fewer projects than the wrong ones. If a project fails any of these, we don’t market it.

  • Building plans approved by the relevant Karachi authority
  • Clear land title that we verify ourselves
  • A developer with completed, lived-in buildings
  • A fixed price and a written instalment schedule
  • Construction progress you can follow from abroad
  • Homes priced for working families

Why off-plan

A home in Karachi, paid for over the build.

Off-plan means you buy before the building is finished and pay in instalments as it is built, which puts a good home within reach of a monthly salary.

Spread the cost

Instalments during construction instead of one large payment up front.

For family, or for later

A home for your parents, for your own return to Karachi, or to rent out.

Someone on your side

We work for buyers abroad: we explain the options, compare projects and follow up with the developer, so you are not managing a purchase from another country alone.

Questions

Is NuraniHomes licensed?

We are applying for our Dubai brokerage licence and RERA approval to market Karachi property from the UAE. Until those are in place, we are only collecting interest, and nothing on this site is an offer to sell.

Who are your developers?

We work with established Karachi developers. We will publish each project, with its approvals, once it is cleared for marketing in the UAE.

Do I need to travel to Karachi?

No. We meet you in Dubai, and our Karachi team handles the paperwork. You are welcome to visit the site whenever you like.

How do payments work?

Each project has its own instalment plan, set by the developer and given to you in writing before you commit.
